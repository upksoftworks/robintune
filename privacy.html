RobinTune privacy policy

RobinTune by UPK Softworks. Effective 21 November 2026.

RobinTune is a sound-making app. It records audio only on your device, keeps everything it makes on your device, and sends nothing anywhere. This page explains exactly what that means.

The microphone

RobinTune asks for permission to use the microphone the first time you tap RECORD. Once you have allowed it, RobinTune uses the microphone only while its SAMPLE screen is open: there it shows how loud the microphone is on a level meter, and it records only when you tap RECORD (until you tap it again, or for at most 60 seconds). The level meter keeps nothing; only recordings are saved, as WAV files on your device. The microphone is switched off when you leave the SAMPLE screen or the app, and RobinTune never listens in the background. If you say no, everything else in the app still works.

What is stored, and where

Your recordings, the sounds, loops and music you save, and the presets you save are stored only on your device, in RobinTune's own folder (Android/data/uk.upksoftworks.robintune). When you open a WAV file with LOAD WAV, RobinTune makes a private copy of that one file to work with. RobinTune does not read any other files on your device.

When you choose EXPORT WAV, RobinTune puts a copy of that sound in your phone's shared Music/RobinTune folder, so that other apps on your phone can use it. On Android 9 and older, Android asks you for permission to write to storage the first time you do this; RobinTune uses that permission only to write those exported files.

You can delete any of the files in RobinTune's own folder on its LIBRARY screen. Uninstalling RobinTune deletes all of them; files you exported to the Music folder stay until you delete them.

What is sent anywhere

Nothing. RobinTune does not have permission to use the internet. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting of its own and no third-party tracking code, and it does not collect, share or sell any personal or usage data.

The only time a file leaves RobinTune is when you choose SHARE: Android then shows its share sheet and the file goes to the app you pick (for example email or cloud storage). What happens to it after that is up to that app and its own privacy policy.

If you have turned on crash reporting in Android or Google Play, Google may send anonymous crash reports about RobinTune to UPK Softworks under Google's own policies. These contain technical details of the crash, never your recordings or files.

Children

RobinTune collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
